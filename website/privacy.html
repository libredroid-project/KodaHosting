Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05 · Applies to the KodaHosting Android app and this website.

Summary

KodaHosting runs Minecraft servers on your own device. Your worlds, configs and logs stay on your phone. We store a minimum of account/status metadata so the tunnel and multi-device features work, and nothing else — unless you explicitly tap "Ask AI" (see section 4).

1. Data stored on your device

Server files, worlds, logs, plugins, configs and your settings are stored locally. They never leave your device unless you export them yourself or use the AI crash assistant (section 4).

2. Account & status metadata (Supabase)

The app stores the following metadata in a Supabase (Supabase Inc.) hosted database, keyed to a random app identifier (app UUID):

  • App installation id, account codes, and (if you link one) your Minecraft username
  • Server status heartbeat: online/offline state, player count, server software and version
  • Optional device diagnostics: device model, Android version, RAM, CPU cores, battery level, network type — used for support and capacity planning
  • Optional nickname you choose in the app

This data is not world content, chat, or player data. It cannot be used to see what happens on your server. Supabase processes it on our behalf; see supabase.com/privacy.

3. Tunnel

Player connections are relayed through a frp tunnel operated for KodaHosting. The relay sees connection metadata (IP address, connection duration and volume) required to forward traffic. Game content is not inspected or stored.

4. AI-assisted crash analysis (optional, user-initiated)

When you tap "Ask AI" on a crash screen, the app sends:

  • the last ~1000 lines of your server log (which may contain player names and IP addresses), and
  • basic device information (model, RAM, Android version) and server settings (software, version, RAM allocation).

…to OpenRouter (OpenRouter.ai), which processes the request through the Google Gemma model. Recipients: OpenRouter and (as the underlying model provider) Google. These services are located in the United States; data may be processed there.

  • This happens only when you tap "Ask AI" — never automatically. The first use asks for your explicit consent.
  • Usage is limited to 10 requests per day.
  • Requests on the free tier may be used by the model provider for training per OpenRouter's terms — do not use the feature if you are not comfortable with this.
  • We cannot delete data already sent to OpenRouter; their retention applies.
  • AI answers are automated suggestions, not professional support.

5. Legal basis (GDPR)

Account/status metadata: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — operating the service you asked for. AI crash analysis: your explicit consent by pressing the button (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); you can withdraw it any time by simply not using the feature.

6. Retention

Device-local data lives until you delete the app or the server. Status metadata is kept while your account is active; deleting your account (or asking us to) removes the related rows. AI requests are retained by OpenRouter/Google per their policies — we hold no copies.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your stored metadata, or object to processing, by contacting us (see imprint / repository). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Children

KodaHosting is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 where local law requires). We do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes are also announced in the app.

10. Contact

See the imprint details in the app (Licenses → Imprint) or open an issue at github.com/libredroid-project/KodaHosting.
