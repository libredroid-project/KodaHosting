AI Crash Analysis: Google Gemma via OpenRouter
===============================================

Feature: "Ask AI" crash analysis (user-initiated, max 10 requests/day).

Model: Google Gemma (google/gemma-4-31b-it:free)
- Gemma is provided by Google under the Gemma Terms of Use
  (https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms) and Gemma Prohibited Use Policy.
- Inference is provided through OpenRouter (https://openrouter.ai),
  an independent API gateway. See OpenRouter's Terms of Service and
  Privacy Policy on their website.

Data notice: when the user taps "Ask AI", the last server log lines and
basic device information (model, RAM, Android version) are sent to
OpenRouter for analysis. Nothing is sent automatically. See the app's
Privacy Policy for details.

The AI response is an automated suggestion, NOT guaranteed-correct
support. The user is responsible for reviewing and applying any change.
